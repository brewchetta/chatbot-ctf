# Press Releases

## Pentagon Confirms It Still Has Exactly Five Sides
Officials reported this morning that a full survey was completed and the building remains pentagonal. "We counted twice," said a spokesperson, "and then once more for morale."

## New Initiative: Meetings Reduced By 3%
A bold new plan will replace 3% of meetings with emails that could have been meetings. Analysts say this is the most significant reform since the invention of the stapler.

## Great Hallway Shortage of 2026 Resolved
Following reports that hallways had become "a little too long," the Department has introduced scooters. Helmets are mandatory. Wheelie policy is under review.
